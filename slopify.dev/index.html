01 — What this is

The ideomotor effect of software

Slopify started as an experiment: a single repo with the rules taken off. Anyone could add anything, in anything. Predictably, the result was a massive shitshow — which was the point. Nobody steered it, and it went somewhere anyway.

That is the whole thesis. Like a Ouija board planchette, or like Reddit’s r/place, the thing moves because a lot of small unconscious pushes add up. Slopify is that, for code.

The goal is not to make good software. The goal is not to make bad software. The goal is to make software, continuously, in public, and see what the pile becomes. Hence: slop.

02 — How to participate

Six steps, one of which is thinking

Everything happens on GitCamp, the open-source git host built by SimuCorps. It is free, it takes a minute, and it is the only way in.

  1. Make a GitCamp account

    Head to gitcamp.net and sign up. GitCamp is AGPL-3.0 and self-hostable — leaving is a git clone, not a support ticket.

  2. Fork Slopify

    Fork SimuCorps/Slopify, then clone your fork locally.

  3. Build something stupid

    Put it in slopify.dev/season-1/your-thing/. Any language, any framework, any size. One file or forty.

  4. Submit the contribution

    Open a pull request from your fork back into main on GitCamp. Describe it or don’t.

  5. Watch it land

    Merged contributions ship to this site under the current season. Your slop is now permanent, public record.

  6. Come back and ship more

    Read what everyone else built. Get worse ideas. Repeat until the season closes.

03 — The rules

There are two of them, really

Ship it through GitCamp, and don’t make it a crime. Everything past that is fair game.

Allowed

  • Any programming language, including ones you invented
  • Any framework, runtime, or build system
  • Any feature, however unrelated to the last one
  • Any level of seriousness — production-grade or two lines of nonsense
  • AI-generated code, wholesale. It’s called Slopify
  • Breaking things other people built, if it’s funny
  • Contributing every single day

Not allowed

  • Anything illegal
  • Malware, miners, or exfiltration of any kind
  • Harassment, or targeting real people
  • Secrets, credentials, or other people’s private data
04 — Seasons

Slop ships in batches

Each season is its own subdirectory and its own clean slate. When a season closes, it freezes as an artifact of whatever the crowd was collectively thinking that round.

Season 1

The first batch under the new rules, and the first one hosted entirely on GitCamp. Everything merged this season lands in slopify.dev/season-1/.

05 — Bounties

We pay you, not the other way around

Slopify is funded by SimuCorps. There is nothing to buy here and no tier to subscribe to. Money moves outward: we put cash behind contributions we like.

Season prizes

Cash awarded at the close of each season to the contributions that best embody the spirit of the thing.

Spot bounties

Posted mid-season for specific dumb ideas we want to see exist. First working submission takes the payout.

Wildcard

Discretionary payouts for anything that makes the team stop and stare. No criteria, no appeals.

How to be eligible: have a merged contribution in the current season on GitCamp. That’s the entire qualification process. Payout details and amounts are posted in the Discord.
