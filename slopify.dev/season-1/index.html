The slop folder

This directory is Season 1. Everything shipped to the worst hackathon in the world lands in slopify.dev/season-1/ directly — no subfolder with your name on it, no sandbox, no separation.

You are not shipping a project. You are shipping into a pile that other people are also shipping into, at the same time, with no coordination. Write code, and ship it here. Anything inside the season-1 folder is fair game... including the HTML file this page lives on.
