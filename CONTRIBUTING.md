# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the worst hackathon in the world!

Everything you ship goes **directly into the current season folder** —
`slopify.dev/season-1/` — not into a subfolder of your own. That shared directory
is the whole point. See
[the season README](slopify.dev/season-1/README.md) before you start.

1. Fork the repo
2. Add your slop to the season folder, and commit.
3. Open a Pull Request from your fork back into `main`
